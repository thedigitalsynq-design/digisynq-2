07.1 • LIFECYCLE PHASES DETERMINISTIC LIFECYCLE

HOW AN INDIVIDUAL SYNQ PROGRESSES.

Every Synq mandate traverses these seven structured operational gates to guarantee counterparty performance, regulatory compliance, and settlement finality.

PHASE 01 AUDIT

VERIFY

Authenticate legal entity identity, unencumbered chain of title, stage dimensions, guild jurisdictions, insurance coverage, and availability windows before intake approval.

Gate: Zero unverified claims
PHASE 02 ROUTING

MATCH

Map requirements against active ecosystem telemetry. Surface optimal counterparty nodes considering acoustic ratings, schedule windows, and tax credit corridors.

Gate: Multi-dimensional compatibility score
PHASE 03 BINDING

COMMIT

Bind bilateral attachments under standardized DigiSynq terms. Lock milestone gates, deposit security escrow, and confirm load-in dates.

Gate: Multi-party commitment locked
PHASE 04 MOBILIZATION

EXECUTE

Mobilize physical capacity. Gear packages dispatch, soundstages load in, art departments build, and principal photography commences on schedule.

Gate: Physical load-in & call sheet activation
PHASE 05 TRACKING

MONITOR

Track daily shoot wrap reports, soundstage power draw, and delivery milestone receipts. Intervene immediately if unexpected operational drift occurs.

Gate: Milestone progress checks
PHASE 06 FINALITY

SETTLE

Disburse escrow funds against objective milestone approvals. Equipment deposits return, payroll cleanses, and fee distribution resolves with zero default track record.

Gate: Escrow settlement finality
PHASE 07 COMPOUNDING DATA

LEARN

Ingest empirical execution telemetry into the DigiSynq Intelligence core. Turnaround cycle times, counterparty reliability scores, and pricing benchmarks refine future matching accuracy.

Outcome: Systemic intelligence compounds
