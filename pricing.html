ESCROW & SETTLEMENT REGULATORY DISCLOSURE

DigiSynq does not operate as a non-banking financial company (NBFC), bank, depository, or escrow agent. All transaction escrow funds are held directly in segregated accounts managed by RBI-regulated Scheduled Commercial Banks and licensed independent escrow trustees. DigiSynq provides the coordination, credential verification, and milestone inspection software layer that triggers verified release instructions.
