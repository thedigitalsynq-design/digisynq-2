Operational Role What DigiSynq Is

A technology communications and state-machine clearing layer matching authenticated capacity with production requirements via deterministic rules.

Telemetry Oracle & Milestone Verifier

Uniform, bilateral institutional terms vetted by entertainment legal councils, reducing weeks of ad-hoc negotiation to minutes of digital execution.

Statutory Demarcation What DigiSynq Is NOT
Not a Bank or Escrow Depository

DigiSynq never takes balance-sheet custody of client funds. All earnest deposits, completion draws, and disbursements are executed strictly by licensed, chartered banking institutions.

Not a Registered Broker-Dealer or Fin-Advisor

We do not syndicate securities, offer debt instruments, or structure tax-incentive equity. Bridge facilities and financing are provided directly by institutional capital partners.

Not a Completion Guarantor or Insurance Carrier

DigiSynq provides deterministic telemetry to completion bond firms and underwriters, but does not underwrite completion bonds or assume primary production insurance risk.
