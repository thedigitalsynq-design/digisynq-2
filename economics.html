Revenue architecture • Node 08

3 primary coordination revenue streams

DigiSynq is asset-light by design. We do not own stages, cinema cameras, or intellectual property. We earn when coordination creates measurable commercial value for productions.

Revenue stream 01

Coordination & transaction fees

A success-based percentage fee charged on matched capacity: stage sub-leases, camera fleet rentals, or crew attachments mobilized through the network.

Status: Live pilots • 1.5% to 4.5% of transaction volume based on project tier.
Revenue stream 02

Settlement & escrow administration

Administrative verification fees for structuring milestone draw schedules, auditing delivery handoffs, and managing payout authorizations with licensed banking partners.

Status: Banking partnership • Flat milestone fee or basis point schedule.
Revenue stream 03

Enterprise intelligence subscriptions

Institutional subscription access to the 7 telemetry pillars: regional capacity maps, content demand mandates, crew reliability benchmarks, and territory release models.

Status: Enterprise licenses • Custom monthly/annual license.

Escrow & settlement regulatory disclosure

DigiSynq does not operate as a non-banking financial company (NBFC), bank, depository, or escrow agent. All transaction escrow funds are held directly in segregated accounts managed by RBI-regulated Scheduled Commercial Banks and licensed independent escrow trustees. DigiSynq provides the coordination, credential verification, and milestone inspection software layer that triggers verified release instructions.
