VALUE MODEL

How DigiSynq Captures Value

account_balance ALIGNMENT-BASED REVENUE MODEL

WE EARN WHEN COORDINATION CREATES VALUE.

DigiSynq is asset-light by design. We do not own stages, cameras, or intellectual property. Our revenue is generated through transparent coordination fees, milestone escrow administration in partnership with licensed financial institutions, and specialized enterprise intelligence subscriptions.

• RECONCILED WITH DIGISYNQ PUBLIC PRICING ARCHITECTURE
3 PRIMARY REVENUE STREAMS
REVENUE STREAM 01

Coordination & Transaction Fees

A percentage fee charged on successfully completed matches: stage sub-leases, camera fleet rentals, or crew attachments mobilized through the network.

STATUS: LIVE PILOTS • 1.5% to 4.5% of transaction volume based on project tier.
REVENUE STREAM 02

Settlement & Escrow Administration

An administrative verification fee for structuring milestone draw schedules, auditing delivery handoffs, and managing payout authorizations with licensed banking partners.

STATUS: PILOT WITH BANKING PARTNERS • Flat milestone fee or basis point schedule.
REVENUE STREAM 03

Enterprise Intelligence Subscriptions

Subscription-based access to the 7 telemetry pillars: regional capacity maps, content demand mandates, crew reliability benchmarks, and territory release insights.

STATUS: ENTERPRISE TIERS (PILOT) • Custom monthly/annual license.
PRICING & SERVICE CATEGORIZATION

WHAT IS FREE, PAID, CUSTOM, AND PILOT

FREE BASIC ACCESS

Creative & Technician Discovery

Creating a profile, logging verified credits, listing available packages, and basic directory discovery are completely free for individual craftspeople.

PAID COORDINATION

Active Synqs & Dispatch

Fees are applied strictly when an active coordination agreement is executed: matching gear, locking soundstages, or securing production attachments.

CUSTOM ENTERPRISE

Studio & Slate Management

Studios and large production houses managing annual slates across multiple regional hubs receive dedicated coordination desks and custom billing agreements.

WORKSHOPS & GUILDS

Ecosystem Programs

Hands-on production masterclasses and peer craft guild alliances operate as specialized ecosystem learning initiatives with program-specific pricing.

REGULATORY & FINANCIAL CUSTODY DISCLOSURE

Third-Party Banking & Escrow Infrastructure

DigiSynq provides coordination technology, milestone verification protocols, and workflow tools. DigiSynq does not operate as a bank, NBFC, or direct escrow custodian. All client funds and production milestone deposits are held by regulated, licensed banking and escrow institutions under mutual tripartite agreements. Payouts are authorized only upon verified milestone completion handoffs.
