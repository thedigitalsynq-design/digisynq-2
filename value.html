08.2 • THE ECONOMIC MODEL OUTCOME ALIGNED

HOW DIGISYNQ EARNS: FOUR ARCHITECTURAL PILLARS.

We do not sell seat licenses or charge upfront tollbooths. Our economic model is strictly aligned with the value created by coordination:

PILLAR 01

ASSET-LIGHT

DigiSynq does not own the soundstages, hold hardware leases, or employ crew. We eliminate capital drag and remain pure coordination infrastructure.

PILLAR 02

STRICTLY NEUTRAL

DigiSynq never competes with participants. We do not produce competing movies, own competing rental houses, or run competing talent representation.

PILLAR 03

OUTCOME-ALIGNED

DigiSynq earns a modest coordination percentage only when a Synq activates and delivers measurable yield, aligning our incentives with successful execution.

PILLAR 04

DATA-ENABLED

Every completed Synq refines system matching accuracy, tightens pricing benchmarks, and reduces cycle time for future ecosystem participants.
