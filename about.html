The Crucial Institutional Questions

Question 01 Market Failure

Why does DigiSynq exist?

Physical film and television production represents one of the world's most capital-intensive, high-velocity operating environments, yet its operational backplane still relies on disconnected SMS threads, unverified intermediary claims, and fragile ad-hoc spreadsheets.

When a premier virtual production LED volume sits idle between tentpole episodic shoots, that carrying capacity evaporates permanently. DigiSynq replaces asymmetric market friction with deterministic, code-enforced protocol coordination.

Question 03 · Operational Boundaries

What does it not do?

Non-Negotiable Guardrails
Not a Talent Agency

We do not manage talent or skim commissions from union wages. We verify guild accreditation and facilitate direct scheduling without intermediary markups.

Not an Asset Owner

We own zero soundstages, camera packages, or lighting trucks. We never prioritize proprietary inventory over partner facilities.

Not a Bank / Escrow Depository

We do not custody balance-sheet funds. All earnest deposits, completion draws, and disbursements are executed through licensed, chartered FDIC/FCA banking partners.

Not a Creative Arbiter

We do not provide script coverage, cast attachments, or editorial notes. We coordinate physical operational infrastructure so filmmakers can execute uninhibited.

Question 05 Hardcoded Neutrality

How does it stay neutral?

DigiSynq's neutrality is hardcoded into its economic architecture and corporate bylaws. Because we own zero physical production assets and represent zero talent rosters, we have zero financial incentive to route a booking to one facility over another.

Matching decisions are governed strictly by technical parameters, calendar availability, and verified reputation scores.
