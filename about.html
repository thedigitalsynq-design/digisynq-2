Question 01

Why does DigiSynq exist?

Physical film and television production represents one of the world's most capital-intensive, high-velocity operating environments, yet its operational backplane still relies on disconnected SMS threads, unverified intermediary claims, and fragile ad-hoc spreadsheets.

When a 25,000 sq ft virtual production LED volume sits idle for 14 days between tentpole episodic shoots, that carrying capacity evaporates permanently. When a line producer burns 3 shooting days hunting for an IATSE-accredited underwater camera crew, the physical contingency budget collapses. DigiSynq replaces asymmetric market friction with deterministic, code-enforced protocol coordination.

Question 02

What does it actually do?

DigiSynq executes a standardized 6-stage lifecycle across eight institutional production pillars:

01 VERIFY — Cryptographically authenticate corporate standing, TPN facility credentials, serialized gear manifests, and guild rosters.
02 MATCH — Isolate exact spatial, acoustic, electrical, and calendar compatibility behind zero-knowledge bilateral privacy gates.
03 COMMIT — Execute standardized digital covenants and fund segregated escrow accounts at chartered depository banking partners.
04 EXECUTE — Ingest operational telemetry: call sheet check-ins, Brompton gen-lock benchmarks, load-in milestones, and wrap logs.
05 SETTLE — Reconcile audit manifests, execute wrap sign-offs, and trigger sub-48-hour automated milestone disbursements.
06 LEARN — Aggregate objective execution metrics into transparent, non-custodial trust ratings and predictive capacity indices.
Question 03

What does it not do?

Not a Talent Agency

We do not manage talent or skim commissions from union wages. We verify guild accreditation and facilitate direct scheduling without intermediary markups.

Not an Asset Owner

We own zero soundstages, camera packages, or lighting trucks. We never prioritize proprietary inventory over partner facilities.

Not a Bank / Escrow Depository

We do not custody balance-sheet funds. All earnest deposits, completion draws, and disbursements are executed through licensed, chartered FDIC/FCA banking partners.

Not a Creative Arbiter

We do not provide script coverage, cast attachments, or editorial notes. We coordinate physical operational infrastructure so filmmakers can execute uninhibited.

Question 04

Who is it for?

  • • Studio & Streamer Executives: Managing slate-wide capacity allocations, cross-territory tax incentive monetizations, and multi-hub facility commitments.
  • • Independent Line Producers & UPMs: Locking certified soundstages, serialized cine packages, and vetted guild crew on budget and on schedule.
  • • Soundstage & Lot Operators: Monetizing 14-to-60 day dark windows between major studio tentpole leases with zero rate-card dilution.
  • • Equipment Rental Houses: Expanding deployment velocity for high-capital cinema camera packages, anamorphic glass, and mobile LED rigs.
  • • Theatrical Post-Production Suites: Filling color grading DI suites, conform pipelines, and Dolby Atmos 7.1.4 mixing schedules.
  • • Financiers & Completion Guarantors: De-risking physical execution with verifiable milestone telemetry and structured escrow draw releases.
Question 05

How does it stay neutral?

DigiSynq's neutrality is hardcoded into its economic architecture and corporate bylaws.

Because we own zero physical production assets and represent zero talent rosters, we have zero financial incentive to route a booking to one facility over another. Matching decisions are governed strictly by technical parameters, calendar availability, and verified reputation scores.
