Ten Pillars of Enterprise Production Assurance

[ENTERPRISE COMPLIANCE STANDARD]
01

Corporate KYB / KYC Verification

Every contracting entity undergoes corporate standing verification, authorized signatory delegation, and beneficial ownership screening.

02

Technical Asset & Stage Telemetry

Physical facilities submit acoustic NC test reports, certified electrical capacity diagrams, and structural load charts before calendar listing.

03

Guild Standing & Roster Sync

Heads of Department and crew keys are verified against active IATSE, DGA, and Teamster union registers to eliminate credential fraud.

04

Fiduciary Depository Escrow

Client funds are held in segregated, bankruptcy-remote trust accounts at chartered FDIC/FCA institutional banking partners.

05

Cryptographic Milestone Telemetry

Milestone disbursements execute only upon verified objective proofs: call sheet check-ins, gen-lock telemetry, and wrap inspection logs.

06

Non-Custodial Dispute Arbitration

Bilateral disputes are arbitrated by independent entertainment legal panels following expedited 7-day standardized evidence protocols.

07

Data Sovereignty & ZK

Zero-knowledge filters ensure sensitive creative scripts, budgets, and talent attachments are never exposed to counterparties.

08

Published Fee Schedules

Every tariff, rate-card fee, and escrow cost is published upfront. Zero hidden brokerage spreads or backchannel kickbacks.

09

Continuous Security Audits

SOC 2 Type II certified platform infrastructure, annual third-party pen tests, and TPN Blue Shield cloud security compliance.

10

Immutable Verification Ledger

Cryptographically sealed audit trails of all contract state transitions, milestone sign-offs, and wrap inspection sign-offs.
