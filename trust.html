Ten Pillars of Enterprise Production Assurance

How DigiSynq systematically eliminates counterparty risk, asset misrepresentation, and settlement friction across global entertainment pipelines.

Pillar 01 • Corporate & Individual Principal Authentication [VERIFIED STANDARD]

Institutional Identity & Corporate Attestation

Every studio entity, production service company, and department head undergoes rigorous KYC/KYB screening: statutory entity standing (Delaware/Companies House), TIN/W-9/W-8BEN-E tax status certification, authorized signatory validation, and beneficial ownership verification before entering network liquidity pools.

VALIDATION PROTOCOL 100% AUDITED
Statutory Entity Registry W-9 / W-8BEN-E Certification Signatory Attestation Depository Account Matching
Pillar 02 [PHYSICAL AUDIT]

Physical Asset & Facility Validation

Physical soundstages, virtual production volumes, and tier-one camera packages require accredited engineering reports, substation transformer load certificates, and serialized optical bench test manifests.

CRITERIA: Serial numbers, Brompton VP benchmarks, NC acoustic ratings.
Pillar 03 [CRYPTOGRAPHIC]

Deterministic Availability Certification

Stage and inventory capacity is locked via immutable digital holds (24h/48h/7d). Unilateral cancellations or double-booking incur non-waivable protocol penalties and instant reputation score markdowns.

CRITERIA: Synchronized digital holds with cryptographic receipt timestamps.
Pillar 04 [STANDARDIZED]

Standardized Bilateral Legal Covenants

Digital bilateral covenants developed with premier entertainment counsel provide standardized clauses for union wrap windows, overtime multipliers, force majeure, and statutory cancellation remedies.

CRITERIA: Pre-vetted bilateral legal frameworks ready for counsel sign-off.
Pillar 05 [REGULATED]

Regulated Depository Escrow Settle

DigiSynq enforces strict non-custodial architecture. Advance earnest deposits, completion draw schedules, and contingency reserves reside exclusively in segregated omnibus accounts at FDIC/FCA-chartered banks.

CRITERIA: Segregated client funds at chartered FDIC/FCA banking partners.
Pillar 06 • Security [ZERO LEAK]

Operational Data Minimization & IP Isolation

We collect strictly operational parameters required to achieve coordination (dimensions, dates, technical requirements, legal standing). We never store unreleased creative scripts or sensitive IP.

CRITERIA: End-to-end encrypted metadata with strict automated deletion horizons.
Pillar 07 • Commercial privacy [CONFIDENTIAL]

Zero-Knowledge Bilateral Clearing Privacy

Facility rate cards and producer budgets are never publicly indexed. Matches occur behind confidential protocol gates, revealing terms only upon mutual bilateral consent.

CRITERIA: Double-blind requirement matching protecting commercial pricing leverage.
Pillar 08 [EMPIRICAL]

Deterministic Reputation Framework

Scoring is calculated strictly from empirical transaction milestones: punctuality, equipment return condition, invoice payment velocity, and technical compliance.

CRITERIA: Deterministic mathematical scoring with public formulas.
Pillar 09 [72H TRIAGE]

Structured 72-Hour Dispute Adjudication

Clear 72-hour mediation pathways for equipment damage, schedule overruns, or specification discrepancies overseen by neutral production ombudsmen.

CRITERIA: Standardized inspection logs with rapid escrow hold procedures.
Pillar 10 [CONFLICT-FREE]

Protocol Neutrality & Asset Independence

DigiSynq does not own camera packages, operate soundstages, or manage talent. We remain a pure, conflict-free coordination infrastructure platform.

CRITERIA: Zero proprietary assets; strictly unbiased algorithmic matching.

Equity & Participation Rules

Tier-1 Induction & Evidence-Based Appeals

New market participants enter with baseline Tier 1 standing. Proof of past industry credits, IATSE/DGA roster standing, TPN facility certifications, or guild memberships instantly fast-tracks qualification standing.

STATUTORY RULE: Zero permanent algorithmic blacklists.
