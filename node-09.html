tune6-STAGE OPERATIONAL STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP)

STAGE 01 • MEASURE (DIAGNOSTIC AUDIT)

48-Parameter Capability & Exposure Audit

Audits physical, legal, and operational availability. Verifies technical specs, equipment certifications, insurance limits, union status, and geographical constraints. Generates the initial Cinema Radar Risk Exposure Index before counterparties interact.

STAGE 02 • CONNECT (GRAPH MATCHING)

Algorithmic Bipartite Resource Matching

Matches qualified demand signals against verified idle supply. Bipartite graph engine checks multi-node dependencies (e.g. stage availability synchronized with DP schedule and lens package). Zero speculative introductions—matches occur only when all prerequisites clear.

STAGE 03 • OPTIMIZE (OPERATIONAL CLARITY)

Enforcement of the 7 Rules of Clarity

Decomposes project execution into concrete Work Breakdown Structures (WBS). Establishes clear ownership, non-negotiable milestones, change-order protocols, and escrow fund deposit criteria. Both parties execute standardized DigiSynq Bilateral Service Agreements.

STAGE 04 • DEPLOY (EXECUTION DISPATCH)

Mobilization & Live Telemetry Tracking

Resources mobilize onto set, location, or digital workflow. Daily Production Reports (DPR) and telemetry feeds monitor milestone pace. If variance exceeds 5%, Cinema Radar triggers proactive coordinator alerts before delays compound.

STAGE 05 • LEARN (POST-WRAP AUDITING)

Yield Variance & Root-Cause Analysis (RCA)

Post-delivery verification triggers immediate escrow disbursement. The coordination team executes variance analysis (budget-to-actual, schedule delta) and files Root Cause Analyses on any encountered frictions to continuously optimize network models.

STAGE 06 • REPEAT (RECURSIVE INTELLIGENCE)

Network Compounding & Liquidity Enhancement

Verified performance data updates participant reliability scores. Successful pairings establish pre-cleared fast-track protocols for subsequent productions, permanently reducing market friction across ecosystem participants.

shieldGOVERNANCE, MILESTONE ESCROW & CIRCUIT BREAKERS

01. Defined Responsibilities

Every party's scope is itemized in a Single-Accountability Matrix. Ambiguity in role definition results in automatic Stage 03 rejection.

02. Concrete Milestones

Delivery dates are mapped to objective technical proof (e.g. verified raw footage ingest, soundstage wrap sign-off, color grade export).

03. Transparent Prerequisites

Upstream dependencies must clear before milestone timer starts. Counterparties cannot be penalized for upstream partner delays.

04. Fixed Commercial Remuneration

Remuneration schedules are locked into neutral escrow accounts. No unexpected back-end fee deductions or post-delivery renegotiations.

05. Formal Variance Management

Scope changes require a bilateral Change Order signed by both key stakeholders with adjusted budget and timeline allocations.

06. Dual-Signoff Escrow Release

Neutral capital escrow releases funds automatically upon technical milestone verification and counterparty concurrence within 24 hours.

CIRCUIT BREAKER PROTOCOL • DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Level 1 Mediation (< 24 hrs): Automated pause on milestone clock. DigiSynq coordination triage verifies physical DPRs, raw logs, and timecards to resolve factual variance.
Level 2 Technical Panel (< 72 hrs): Independent 3-person artisan arbitration panel renders a final binding deliverable verdict. Historical DigiSynq dispute rate remains below 0.18%.
