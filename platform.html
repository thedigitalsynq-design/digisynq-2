Layer 03 • Commit [System protocol]

Standardized Covenants & Depository Escrow

Execution of standardized bilateral production covenants, immutable schedule locks, and milestone payment tranches routed into regulated partner bank escrow depositories.

Framework: Bilateral e-signatures & licensed partner bank escrow API triggers.
Layer 04 • Surveillance [System protocol]

Operational Dependency Surveillance

Active milestone surveillance spanning prep checkout, stage load-in, call-sheet turnaround compliance, and principal wrap. Algorithmic telemetry detects variance and alerts network coordinators.

Validation: DCI QC reports, optical collimator inspection certs, utility logs.
