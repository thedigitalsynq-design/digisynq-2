Evidentiary Classification & Provenance

Cryptographic Data Provenance Framework

We never label speculative or modeled projections as verified facts. Every data source adheres to strict provenance labeling.

Classification Definition & Evidentiary Standard Verification Method Typical System Usage
[FIRST-PARTY] Direct transactional data generated on the DigiSynq protocol. Cryptographic hash of bilateral sign-off and wrap certificates. Capacity booking logs, check-in timestamps, trust scores.
[PARTNER] Direct telemetry provided by verified facility or financial partners. API integration or authenticated audit document upload. Stage power telemetries, escrow confirmations, credit ratings.
[PUBLIC] Public corporate filings, union collective bargaining agreements, trade logs. Official government registers & published union scale schedules. Guild rate baselines, entity tax registrations, box office records.
[LICENSED] Commercial industry intelligence from accredited research vendors. Authorized commercial licensing agreements. Syndicated streaming viewership and international buyer mandates.
[MODELLED] Algorithmic projections and scenario simulations derived from historical trends. Backtested statistical regressions with explicit variance ranges. Seasonal utilization curves, regional crew demand forecasting.
