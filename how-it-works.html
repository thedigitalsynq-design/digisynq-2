The Six-Stage Deterministic Coordination Engine

Immutable state progression: each stage transitions only upon verified counterparty evidence and regulatory compliance.

Stage 01 of 06 • Cryptographic Authentication [Active protocol]

01 verify

“Authenticate ground truth before asset entry.”

Production schedules frequently collapse under phantom stage availability, uncertified technical claims, and unverifiable crew holds. DigiSynq requires cryptographic entity verification and empirical spec validation before any resource enters the syndication network.

Verified Telemetry Vectors
• Corporate Legal Entities & KYC • NC-25 / NC-30 Acoustic Ratings • Serialized Gear Benchmarks • DGA / IATSE / PGA Credentials • Commercial Liability ($10M)
Operational imperative: Eliminates 94% of pre-production discovery churn and erroneous availability declarations.
Stage 02 of 06 • Multi-Variable Constraint Solving [Active protocol]

02 match

“Deterministic synthesis of physical demand and capacity.”

The DigiSynq constraint engine evaluates multi-dimensional variables—geographical proximity, power transformer capacity, camera optical profiles, union turnaround rules, and counterparty delivery scores—to synthesize optimal pairings.

Matching Parameters
• Sub-Second Constraint Resolution • Multi-Jurisdictional Tax Compliance • Bilateral Solvency Indices • High-Frequency Optical Sync
Institutional neutrality: DigiSynq holds zero balance-sheet equipment or facilities; algorithmic pairing is provably unbiased.
Stage 03 of 06 • Bilateral Covenants & Escrow Depository [Active protocol]

03 commit

“Standardized commercial covenants and capital locking.”

Replaces protracted legal negotiation and multi-week contract redlines with pre-structured bilateral covenants, automated hold progression, and regulated multi-signature escrow tranches.

Commitment Protocols
• Standardized Master Covenants • Programmatic 24h / 48h Holds • Force Majeure & Weather Terms • Bank Escrow Depository Lock
Fiduciary architecture: Mobilization capital locked in chartered banking partner escrow; released strictly upon authenticated milestone certificates.
Stage 04 of 06 • Production Telemetry & Milestone Surveillance [Active protocol]

04 execute

“Surveil operational dependencies and mitigate variance.”

Granular milestone surveillance monitors operational dependencies across prep check-in, load-in telemetry, call sheet turnarounds, and principal wrap. Emerging variances trigger automated contingency re-routing across local network inventory.

Execution Telemetry
• Authenticated Call Sheet Sync • Optical Collimator Bench Tests • Stage Power Staging Telemetry • Automated Contingency Routing
Contingency guarantee: Pre-cleared secondary inventory within 35 miles deployed automatically upon critical equipment exception.
Stage 05 of 06 • Wrap Verification & Net-24H Settlement [Active protocol]

05 settle

“Instant invoice verification and programmatic capital release.”

Settlement latency and carrying costs are eradicated through automated milestone audit, instant invoice reconciliation against contractual covenants, and certified wrap sign-off releases.

Settlement Workflow
• Certified Wrap Sign-Off • Contractual Invoice Reconciliation • Regulated Chartered Bank Payouts • Automated Tax Reporting (1099/TDS)
Regulatory disclosure: Fiduciary custody and fiat disbursements facilitated exclusively through licensed, chartered institutional partners.
Stage 06 of 06 • Adaptive Telemetry & Consensus Reputation [Active protocol]

06 learn

“Accrete empirical performance into predictive intelligence.”

Every completed production injects empirical telemetry back into the protocol—updating bilateral counterparty trust scores, refining power efficiency indices, and training predictive capacity models across global entertainment hubs.

Adaptive Outcomes
• Bilateral Solvency & Trust Accretion • Facility Power Utilization Telemetry • Algorithmic Seasonal Rate Indices • Guild Turnaround Compliance Logs
Institutional confidentiality: Telemetry is cryptographically anonymized and aggregated; proprietary scripts, budgets, and creative IP remain strictly confidential.
