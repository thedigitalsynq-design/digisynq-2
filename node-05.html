MISSIONS

5 Operational Mission Configurations

hub ONE PIPELINE. FIVE OUTCOME MODES.

WHAT CAN THE NETWORK ACCOMPLISH?

Missions are not separate software programs. They are operational configurations of DigiSynq's 6-stage pipeline, designed to achieve five specific industry outcomes: creating content, activating idle capacity, connecting talent, optimizing distribution, or unlocking market intelligence.

• ALL MISSIONS EXECUTE UNDER STANDARDIZED OPERATIONAL PROTOCOLS
MISSION 01 // PRODUCTION CREATION

CREATE

ACTIVE PROTOCOL
TRIGGER A greenlit or development-stage screenplay needs complete crew packaging, stage schedule lock, and milestone financing attachment.
WHAT DIGISYNQ DOES Executes the 6-stage pipeline from Stage 01 (Measure specs) through Stage 04 (Deploy call sheets), structuring the entire creative pod.
OUTPUT Fully packaged production pod, locked shooting dates, verified HOD call sheets, milestone escrow deployed.
WHO USES IT & RELATED PRODUCTS Producers & Studios. Related: Crew Sync, Screenplay Vault, Financier Sync.
MISSION 02 // CAPACITY ACTIVATION

ACTIVATE

ACTIVE PROTOCOL
TRIGGER Soundstages have dark calendar gaps, or rental houses have high-end cinema camera and lighting packages sitting idle.
WHAT DIGISYNQ DOES Identifies calendar availability and matches short-window commercial shoots, pickups, and song units into open capacity.
OUTPUT Booked facility days, higher equipment utilization, zero carrying cost loss.
WHO USES IT & RELATED PRODUCTS Stage lot owners & Rental houses. Related: Asset Utilization, Venue Sync.
MISSION 03 // TALENT MOBILIZATION

CONNECT

ACTIVE PROTOCOL
TRIGGER Experienced technicians and department heads seek verified attachments between feature film cycles.
WHAT DIGISYNQ DOES Verifies credits and portfolio specializations, matching talent to cross-regional slates with guaranteed milestone escrow.
OUTPUT Direct attachment to funded call sheets with clear day-rates and escrow security.
WHO USES IT & RELATED PRODUCTS Directors of Photography, Editors, Sound Designers, Guild Technicians. Related: Talent Revival, Crew Sync.
MISSION 04 // RELEASE OPTIMIZATION

DISTRIBUTE

PILOT COHORT
TRIGGER A finished film master needs transparent windowing, regional theatrical screen access, or digital streaming acquisitions.
WHAT DIGISYNQ DOES Audits territory demand telemetry, structures theatrical release windows, and matches content directly to regional distributors.
OUTPUT Executed theatrical and OTT distribution agreements with verified reporting standards.
WHO USES IT & RELATED PRODUCTS Independent producers, Sales agents, Content financiers. Related: Distribution Intelligence, Demand Pulse.
MISSION 05 // TELEMETRY & DATA

INTELLIGENCE

PILOT COHORT
TRIGGER A studio executive, investor, or producer requires objective market telemetry on regional capacity, audience demand, or cost baselines.
WHAT DIGISYNQ DOES Aggregates verified operational data across 7 telemetry pillars into actionable decision reports.
OUTPUT Territory capacity maps, rate transparency benchmarks, content demand telemetry.
WHO USES IT & RELATED PRODUCTS Studio executives, Media financiers, Institutional investors. Related: 7 Telemetry Pillars, Insights Engine.
