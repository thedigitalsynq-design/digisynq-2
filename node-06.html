THE 7 TELEMETRY PILLARS
PILLAR 01 // PRODUCTION ACTIVITY

Market Signal

DATA SOURCE: Filming permit registers, call sheets, trade notices.
WHAT IS MEASURED: Active production volume by city, genre, budget tier.
HOW VERIFIED: Confirmed stage bookings & guild attachments.
WHAT USER GETS: Macro visibility on where production volume is surging or contracting.
PILLAR 02 // ACQUISITION APPETITE

Content Demand

DATA SOURCE: Theatrical distributor acquisition desks & OTT procurement mandates.
WHAT IS MEASURED: Open buyer genre windows, target runtimes, language dub needs.
HOW VERIFIED: Direct buyer RFPs & confirmed platform slate mandates.
WHAT USER GETS: Targeted packaging direction aligned with confirmed buyer appetite.
PILLAR 03 // FACILITY AVAILABILITY

Capacity Map

DATA SOURCE: Soundstage facility management systems & rental lot calendars.
WHAT IS MEASURED: Dark calendar windows, lot square footage, acoustic ratings.
HOW VERIFIED: Direct owner calendar integrations & daily occupancy checks.
WHAT USER GETS: Immediate discovery of vacant stage days at negotiated short-run rates.
PILLAR 04 // TERRITORIAL TRACTION

Audience Pulse

DATA SOURCE: Regional advance ticketing inquiries, fan communities, search trends.
WHAT IS MEASURED: Territory-by-territory viewer interest before release.
HOW VERIFIED: Verified engagement thresholds & sample demographic polling.
WHAT USER GETS: Data-backed screen allocation advice and targeted marketing guidance.
PILLAR 05 // LIQUIDITY FLOW

Capital Flow

DATA SOURCE: Media funds, completion lenders, co-production entities.
WHAT IS MEASURED: Available debt and equity allocations seeking production attachments.
HOW VERIFIED: Verified escrow reserves & formal institutional commitments.
WHAT USER GETS: Direct insight into active financial counterparties for specific budget ranges.
PILLAR 06 // HUMAN RESOURCE METRICS

Crew Telemetry

DATA SOURCE: Verified call sheet participation & post-wrap peer audits.
WHAT IS MEASURED: On-time call sheet reliability, overtime adherence, specialization.
HOW VERIFIED: Producer wrap sign-offs and peer guild review checks.
WHAT USER GETS: Objective reliability scores replacing word-of-mouth gossip.
PILLAR 07 // PIPELINE COMPLETION SPEED

Post Throughput

DATA SOURCE: VFX facility capacity, DI suite schedules, Atmos audio stages.
WHAT IS MEASURED: Turnaround latency from rough cut to final delivery master.
HOW VERIFIED: Delivery QC certificates & platform inspection handoffs.
WHAT USER GETS: Accurate post-production scheduling and elimination of release delays.
